Procurement & Partnership FAQ

Essential guidelines on factory capabilities, certification dossiers, minimum orders, and customized thermoformed fiber tooling.

What products does notch manufacture?expand_more

We engineer molded sugarcane bagasse and bamboo fiber tableware including round plates, clamshells, deep soup & salad bowls, multi-compartment bento trays, and custom bespoke packaging formats.

What are your minimum order quantities (MOQ)?expand_more

Our standard minimum order is direct export FCL ocean containers (1x 20ft or 40ft HQ), with pallet quantities (1–5 pallets) available for select domestic buffered warehouse SKUs.

Are all products certified compostable and PFAS-free?expand_more

Yes. All catalog items are 100% plant-based, certified BPI Compostable, EN 13432 compliant, FDA food-contact approved, and manufactured completely 100% PFAS-free.

How fast can you dispatch samples?expand_more

Free physical evaluation sample boxes are packed and dispatched within 24 hours directly to your test kitchen or office via international air express.

Can you produce custom shapes and embossed logos?expand_more

Yes. We offer turnkey bespoke tooling, proprietary 3D CAD modeling, brand logo embossing, and variable wall-thickness thermoforming molds with rapid prototyping turnaround.
